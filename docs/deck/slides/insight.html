02 · USER INSIGHT

Today's workarounds stitch three apps and a phone call together and still end in guessing; data, not UI, is the bottleneck

CURRENT WORKAROUNDPAIN POINTEVIDENCE

INSIGHT 1

The meal is the anchor. Parking is a dependent decision, so plan it inside the dining flow.

INSIGHT 2

Legality beats proximity. One tow destroys trust; legal-only must be a hard rule.

INSIGHT 3

Walk tolerance varies by group. Elderly parents ≠ friends. Use real road distance.

INSIGHT 4

Data, not UI, is the bottleneck. Price and capacity are missing; honesty beats invention.
