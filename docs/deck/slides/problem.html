01 · PROBLEM & MOTIVATION

Delhi diners can book a table in seconds but cannot plan legal parking for it, costing time, fines and lost restaurant visits

TARGET USERS (PERSONAS)

The family host · 35–55

Weekend dinner at CP or Khan Market with parents and kids. Pain: long walks and roadside towing. Values: certainty over price.

The young couple / friends · 22–34

Evening plans in Hauz Khas or Lajpat Nagar. Pain: circling, unknown lot fees, lots closing before they leave. Values: speed and cost.

JOB TO BE DONE

"When I plan a meal out by car, I want to know where I can legally park, what it costs and how far I'll walk, so I arrive on time without a challan or a tow."

WHY IT MATTERS

20.7 Lprivate cars registered in Delhi1; that's the addressable base
~20 minaverage time drivers spend searching for parking (20-city survey incl. New Delhi)2
~30%of urban congestion in Indian cities is cars cruising for parking3
1 in 5mapped Delhi car parks are private or customers-only (205 of 934)4

Sources: 1 Delhi Statistical Handbook 2023 (2.07 M private cars, 31 Mar 2023) · 2 IBM Global Parking Survey, 2011 · 3 Centre for Science & Environment, cited in Swarajya "India's parking woes" · 4 My analysis of the OpenStreetMap Delhi extract, 14 Nov 2026
