06 · PROTOTYPE & LEARNINGS

The prototype works end to end on real Delhi data; building it surfaced six lessons that shape the roadmap · parkwise-delhi.vercel.app

END-TO-END · NO LOGIN
1 · INFRA RISK

Free live map API (Overpass) kept failing with timeouts and 502s, even in production. Fix: bundled a Delhi OpenStreetMap snapshot. Lesson: never put a free third-party API on the critical path.

2 · TRUST

Early "demo" sample data made every result look fake. Fix: real data only, gaps labelled "fee unknown". Lesson: honest uncertainty earns more trust than fake precision.

3 · LEGALITY

205 of 934 lots were private or customers-only and would have been recommended. Fix: eligibility filter before ranking. Lesson: encode non-negotiables as hard rules.

4 · AI UX

An AI box plus a full form felt like forced AI. Fix: one sentence first; the form appears only for review. Lesson: AI should remove steps, not add one.

5 · SILENT ASSUMPTIONS

"Dinner at khaan market" picked Khanna Market and a default time. Fix: typo-tolerant matching + asking for missing time. Lesson: ask, don't assume, on critical inputs.

6 · DATA GAP = MOAT

Only 4 of 934 lots list a price; Khan Market has few mapped lots. Lesson: the product's defensibility is the data layer: operator partnerships and crowd-sourced prices.
