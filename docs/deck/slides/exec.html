EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A meal-first, AI-assisted parking planner can remove Delhi's "where do I park?" problem using open data today, and monetise through restaurants first

1

Problem. Delhi has ~20.7 L private cars, and drivers spend ~20 min looking for parking. No product helps a diner plan legal parking around a meal.

2

Insight. Diners decide the restaurant first; legality matters more than proximity; walk tolerance differs by group; the real gap is missing data (4 of 934 lots list a price).

3

Solution. An AI planner: one sentence in → restaurant + legal car park + real walking route out. Chosen over sensors, satellite counting and booking on value for effort.

4

AI. On-device language model understands intent and asks for missing details; a deterministic geo-engine supplies facts, so it never invents a car park.

5

Proof. Working prototype on 3,281 restaurants and 934 car parks: parkwise-delhi.vercel.app

6

Ask. 90-day Connaught Place pilot; scale if North Star ≥ 500 parked outings/week with zero illegal recommendations.

Source: Delhi Statistical Handbook 2023; IBM Global Parking Survey 2011; OpenStreetMap Delhi extract (14 Nov 2026)
